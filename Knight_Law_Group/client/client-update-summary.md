## 2. New Follow-Up Cadence for Missed (Incomplete) Leads

When a caller doesn't finish — they didn't answer, didn't have time to talk, or hung up before we finished — they're marked an **Incomplete Lead** and automatically enter this friendly follow-up. Everything runs in the lead's language (English or Spanish).

**How it works:**

1. We wait **24 hours** after the lead is marked Incomplete.
2. If they still haven't responded, the follow-up begins.
3. **Days 1–3 (each day):** Alice calls in the **morning**, we send **one text** and **one email**, and Alice calls once more in the **early evening**.
4. **Day 4 onward:** The same set — **a call, a text, and an email** — goes out **every other day**.
5. We keep this up until the lead **replies, picks up and finishes their info, or opts out**. The moment they respond or text **STOP**, all follow-ups stop right away.

All outreach only goes out during normal daytime and early-evening hours.

## 5. How the AI Agent (Alice) Handles Each Call

When Alice reaches a lead, she confirms they're having vehicle trouble, then asks a few quick questions to see how we can help:

- Was the vehicle purchased or leased from a **California** dealership?
- Do you **still have** the vehicle?
- What's the **make and year**?
- For stronger cases: Was it **new or used** (and certified pre-owned)? Have you **taken it in for repairs**? Are you the **owner**?

If a caller is speaking Spanish, Alice automatically continues the whole call in Spanish. Every call ends in one of three outcomes:

### ✅ Retainer Lead — *we sign them*
The vehicle qualifies: bought in California, **2020 or newer**, an eligible make, still owned, repairs attempted, and the caller is the owner. Alice confirms their name, phone, and email, then **sends the retainer agreement to sign** and lets them know their documents are on the way.

### 🤝 Non-Retainer Lead — *still worth a consultation*
A valid situation that isn't an immediate sign-up — for example the make isn't on our priority list, repairs haven't been attempted yet, or the caller isn't the owner. Alice sets them up with a **consultation booking link** to speak with an Intake Analyst.

### ❌ Bad Lead — *doesn't qualify*
Alice politely explains we can't move forward, and why. The most common reasons:

- Vehicle was purchased **out of state** (not California)
- Vehicle is **2019 or older** (we handle 2020 and newer)
- The caller **no longer has** the vehicle (for certain manufacturers)
- Bought **used and not Certified Pre-Owned**
- The vehicle has **no current issues**
- The caller **doesn't wish to pursue** it

### ⏳ Didn't finish?
If a caller is busy or hangs up before we're done, they're marked an **Incomplete Lead** and automatically enter the follow-up cadence in Section 2 above.
