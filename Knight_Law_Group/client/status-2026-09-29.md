## Call notes

Every call now adds a note to the GHL contact, in the **Notes** tab. The "Transcript of the Call" field only shows the latest call; the notes keep all of them. Each note has bold labels so it is easy to scan:

> **Alice Intake · Outbound · Sep 29, 2026 7:15 AM PT**
> **Call:** Spoke to John · 1 min 25 s
> **Result:** Non-Retainer Lead
> **Callback:** requested for 09-30-2026 11:00 AM PT *(only when one was asked for)*
> **Language:** English
> **Summary:** Caller confirmed transmission issues with a 2023 Honda Civic...
> **Recording:** link to the call recording
> **Transcript**
> **User:** Hello?
> **Agent:** Hi, this is Alice with Knight Law Group...
> *Call ID, agent and processing reference, for our team if a call ever needs checking.*

- Calls with Alice Retainer use the same layout, with **Outcome** (for example "Callback scheduled for...", "Signed on the call") and **Objection** in place of Result.
- Voicemails and unanswered calls get a short note (who answered and how long), without a summary or transcript.

## Tags

When a lead has a real conversation with Alice, the contact gets a tag:

| Tag | Added when |
|---|---|
| **alice intake: spoke** | The lead talked with Alice Qualifying (intake): a person answered, it was not a voicemail or no answer, the lead said at least two things and the call lasted at least 15 seconds. |
| **alice retainer: spoke** | The lead talked with Alice Closer (retainer): a person answered, not a voicemail or no answer. |

- Tags are only added, never removed. Once a lead has spoken to Alice, the tag stays.
- Voicemails, missed calls and pick-up-and-hang-up calls do not add a tag.
- Use them in a GHL smart list or filter to see which leads actually spoke with Alice. The notes on the contact show what was said on each call.
