## 1. New criteria: lease and Tesla ("Requires Arbitration")

Alice follows the new intake script.

- **New question after the California question:** "Did you purchase the vehicle, or lease it?" If it was leased, she asks: "And is it still leased, or did you later buy it out?"
- **If the lead says "it's financed" or "I'm making payments",** she asks once: "Is that a lease, or a loan to buy it?"
- **Still leased Honda, Acura, BMW, Mercedes-Benz or Mini:** Bad Lead, reason **Requires Arbitration**.
- **Leased, then bought out:**
  - Honda or Acura: Bad Lead, **Requires Arbitration**.
  - BMW, Mercedes-Benz or Mini: **Non-Retainer** (after the possession and year checks).
- **Tesla (bought or leased):** Bad Lead, **Requires Arbitration**.
- **Arbitration explanation:** Alice reads the approved lines word for word, in English or Spanish:
  - Lease: "Since your vehicle is leased, the manufacturer is actually a party to your lease agreement..."
  - Lease buyout: "Since your vehicle started out as a lease, the manufacturer was a party to that original lease agreement..."
  - Tesla: "Tesla's purchase and lease agreements include a clause requiring disputes to go through arbitration..."
- **If the lead corrects themselves** after hearing it (for example "actually, I bought it out last year"), Alice re-checks the case instead of repeating the rejection.
- **Reworded question** for leases too: "Was the vehicle new or used when you got it?" A leased car can be Certified Pre-Owned, so the CPO check applies to leases as well.
- **New hand-off line** before the retainer walkthrough: "Great, we can move forward. I'll text and email you the representation agreement to sign..."
- **Data to GHL and Salesforce:**
  - **Bad Lead Reason** is sent as **Requires Arbitration**.
  - A new value, **Purchase Type** (Purchased / Leased / Leased, then purchased), is also sent.

## 2. Callback requests (Alice Qualifying)

- When a lead says "I'm driving, call me back", Alice asks when, and confirms the day and time: "Perfect, we'll give you a call tomorrow at 5 PM."
- **Within the 3-day follow-up window:** we call at that time and the regular follow-up waits.
- **Outside the window** (for example "next week"): Alice says "Our team will contact you soon", and the regular follow-up continues, as you asked.
- **Calls only go out between 8 AM and 9 PM Pacific.** If the lead asks for 7 AM, Alice asks for a time in that window.
- The lead can change the time before hanging up, and Alice confirms the new one.

## 3. Live transfer for non-retainer leads

- **Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Pacific:** a lead who qualifies for a consultation is offered a transfer to an Intake Analyst right away: "One of our Intake Analysts is available right now... Would you like me to connect you now?"
- **Outside those hours, or if the lead says no,** they get the consultation booking link as today.
- **If no one picks up at intake,** Alice apologizes and sends the booking link.
- **Lead Status stays Non-Retainer Lead.** A new value, **Non-Retainer Live Transfer = Yes**, tells GHL not to put transferred leads into the regular follow-up.

## 4. A more natural Alice (Alice Qualifying)

From reviewing real calls:

- **She doesn't ask what the lead already told her.** If the lead says "I bought it brand new" or "it's been at the dealer three times", she skips those questions.
- **Natural lead-ins:** she leads into each question naturally ("So, quick question...") instead of firing questions one after another.
- **Questions during the call:** Alice answers, checks in ("Does that help?", worded differently each time), then returns to where she left off.
- **Unclear answers are clarified before deciding:**
  - A "No" to "new or used?" gets a neutral "Sorry, was it new or used?" instead of a guess.
  - An unclear answer about CPO is clarified before a lead is turned away.
- **Better Spanish:**
  - Natural Latin American Spanish.
  - Correct wording for the CPO question ("seminuevo" was being used, which only means used).
  - Correct wording for the text-and-email consent question.
- **Voicemail menus:** Alice now hangs up on a full mailbox or a voicemail menu instead of talking over it for minutes.
- **Pace and timing:**
  - She waits less before answering.
  - Background noise and short "yes / okay" interrupt her less.
  - She speaks at a steady speed, slightly slower only when confirming the name and email.

## 7. Next Best Action (follow-up timing)

- **When it applies:** a lead needs a follow-up but **did not give a specific time.** Next Best Action then picks the best time for our next **call**, **text** and **email**.
- **When it doesn't:** if the lead **did** give a specific time, that is a callback and we call at that exact time.
- **Missed calls:** the next call is tried at a **different time of day** (morning, then midday, then evening), not the same hour every day.
- **Leads who picked up:** it goes by what they said ("I'm at work" → tomorrow evening; "my spouse needs to see it" → tomorrow evening; "call me later" → later the same day).
- **Contact limits:**
  - Everything goes out between **8 AM and 9 PM Pacific**.
  - "No calls / no texts / no emails" preferences are respected.
- **Where to see it:** each call note in GHL shows the planned times and the reason.

- **Do-not-contact flags:** if a lead says "texts and emails are fine, but no calls", it is recorded and respected.
