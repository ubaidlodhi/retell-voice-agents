# Sage & Willow Spa — Service Descriptions

## Signature Massage

The Signature Massage is a **customized treatment** that blends different massage techniques based on what your body needs that day. The therapist mixes Swedish, deep tissue, and **Ashiatsu (walk-on-back)** techniques depending on whether you want to focus on relaxation, tension relief, or both.

**Best for:** clients who aren't sure which style they want, or who want the therapist to adapt to how they're feeling that day.

---

## Swedish Massage

A gentle, relaxing **full-body massage** using long, flowing strokes to reduce stress, improve circulation, and promote overall relaxation. The pressure is light to medium — soothing rather than intense.

**Best for:** first-time massage clients, anyone who finds deeper pressure uncomfortable, or anyone who just wants to unwind.

---

## Deep Tissue Massage

A focused massage using **firm pressure** to target deep muscle layers, helping relieve chronic tension, muscle tightness, and pain — especially in the back, shoulders, and neck. The therapist adjusts pressure to your comfort.

**Best for:** people with chronic muscle pain, athletes, or anyone with significant tension that lighter massage doesn't reach.

---

## Hot Stone Massage

A deeply relaxing massage that uses **heated stones** combined with massage techniques to ease muscle tension, improve circulation, and promote deep relaxation.

**Best for:** colder days, deep relaxation, or anyone who finds heat soothing.

---

## Eastern Therapeutic Ritual

An indulgent therapeutic experience inspired by traditional Eastern wellness. **Warm herbal medicated oil** is paired with **heated stones** and focused techniques to help release muscle tension and create a deeply soothing, restorative experience. Pressure is adjusted to your comfort, with special attention to areas of tension and tenderness.

**Please note:** the herbal oil contains camphor, menthol, and methyl salicylate. It is **not recommended for sensitive, irritated, broken, or damaged skin**, and is for external use only.

**Best for:** clients who want a deeper, warming treatment for tension and soreness, or who enjoy heat and herbal oils.

---

## Prenatal Massage

A gentle, soothing massage **designed specifically for expecting mothers** to help relieve muscle tension, reduce stress, and improve overall comfort during pregnancy. **Available from the second trimester onward (after 12 weeks).** Consulting a doctor before booking is recommended.

**Best for:** pregnant clients from the second trimester (after 12 weeks).

---

## Lymphatic Drainage Massage

A **gentle, light-pressure** massage that stimulates the lymphatic system to help reduce swelling, support detoxification, and promote overall wellness.

**Best for:** clients dealing with swelling, post-surgery recovery, or anyone interested in wellness-focused work rather than tension relief.

---

## 30 Minute Focus Massage

A **thirty-minute** massage that can be focused on your preferred areas (like the neck, shoulders, back, or feet) or used as a quick full-body relaxation session.

**Best for:** people on a tight schedule, lunch breaks, or clients who want spot treatment for a specific tight area.

---

## Couples Massage

A couples massage is two massages booked for the same time slot in our dedicated **couples massage room** — both massages run side by side. The spa confirms each guest's massage details when it reaches out ahead of the appointment.

**Best for:** couples, friends, or family who want to relax together.

---

## Add-On Enhancements

Small extras that can be added to a massage to deepen the experience.

**Available on:** Signature, Swedish, Deep Tissue, Hot Stone, and 30 Minute Focus massages. **Not available on** the Eastern Therapeutic Ritual, Prenatal, Lymphatic Drainage, or Couples massages.

### Herbal Therapy Enhancement

A warm herbal therapy treatment added to your massage.

### Hot Stone Enhancement

Heated stones added to your massage to help relax tight muscles, improve circulation, and deepen relaxation.

### Aromatherapy

Essential oils added to your massage to enhance relaxation, reduce stress, and improve overall mood and wellbeing.

### Steam Eye Mask

A warm steam eye mask placed during your massage to help relieve eye strain, reduce stress, and enhance relaxation.

### Foot Scrub

A relaxing foot treatment that includes exfoliation, massage, and warm stones to soften skin, relieve tension, and improve circulation.
