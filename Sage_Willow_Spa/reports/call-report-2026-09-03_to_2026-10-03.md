## Notes

1. **Callbacks are bringing in business.** 2 of the 7 bookings came from Aria ringing back people who hung up early.
2. **The 3 failed look-ups were all one caller (808-292-4255), Sep 28–29.** Their booking was saved under a second contact with the same phone number. Since Oct 1, look-ups search every contact with that number, and also show bookings from the last 24 hours.
3. **No reschedules yet.** Every caller who changed plans cancelled instead.
4. **17 inbound calls ended at the greeting.** That's common for spa lines (people hang up on hearing an assistant, or dialled by mistake). Those numbers get the automatic callback.
5. **Worth a look (not changed):**
   - **Late-night callback.** Website-form callbacks go out the moment the form arrives, even at night. A form sent at 1:44 AM on Oct 3 got a call at 1:44 AM; it went to voicemail. A quiet-hours rule, such as holding night-time forms until 9 AM, could avoid that.
   - **Look-up error on Oct 2.** On one call, Aria's first appointment look-up was sent without the caller's number and errored. She recovered by asking for the number they booked with and found the appointment.
