## Call 1: Two people, different massages

**Covers:** two-person booking, each guest choosing their own massage and length, asking for the guest's real name, the price total, and the payment and gift card answers.

1. "Hi, I'd like to book massages for me and my wife."
2. You: **Swedish, 60 minutes**. Your wife: **Deep Tissue, 90 minutes**.
3. Give a weekday next week, afternoon. Pick one of the times she offers.
4. **Before giving names**, ask: "Can I pay with Venmo?" Then: "Do you sell gift cards?"
5. When she asks for names, give yours. When she asks for your wife's, first say just **"my wife"**, then give a real name (Jane Doe).
6. Give phone **415-555-0181**. Say yes at the readback.

**Listen for:**
- She only offers times when **two therapists are free together**, side by side.
- **Payment:** "cash, card, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal". She must not say "cash or card only".
- **Gift cards:** e-gift cards on the website, physical gift cards at the spa.
- After those answers she goes back to the booking without losing where she was.
- "My wife" is not accepted as a name. She asks for the real name.
- The readback names both guests and both massages. The **total = Swedish 60 + Deep Tissue 90** (not one price doubled).
- "You're all set" at the end, and two bookings on the test Wix calendar with two different therapists.

## Call 2: Lookup, cancel one, move the other (same number)

**Covers:** appointment lookup with more than one booking, cancel, reschedule, and the tipping answer.

1. "Hi, I want to check my appointments." Give **415-555-0181** if asked.
2. She should find **both** bookings from call 1 (yours and your wife's).
3. "Please cancel my wife's appointment."
4. "And can you move mine to the next day, same time?" Accept a time she offers.
5. Before hanging up: "Can I tip with Zelle?"

**Listen for:**
- Both appointments are found, and the soonest is mentioned first.
- Only your wife's booking is cancelled. Yours is moved, not cancelled.
- **Tipping:** yes, cash, card, Venmo, Zelle or PayPal.

## Call 4: Recording first, then Couples Massage (request first)

**Covers:** Aria waits through an automated recording (Nicky's request 2), and a service Nicky has to approve goes in as a request, not a confirmed booking.

On the test site, "Couples Massage" is a stand-in I set up to behave like Nicky's real request-first service.

1. **As soon as the call connects**, read this in a flat, recorded-message voice, without stopping between sentences: *"Thank you for calling. This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes. For sales, press 1. For support, press 2. Please stay on the line."* Then stay silent for 3 seconds.
2. Now talk normally: "Hi, sorry about that. I'd like a couples massage for me and my husband."
3. Pick a day and a time she offers. Give names (yours and his) and phone **415-555-0183**. Confirm the readback.

**Listen for:**
- **During the recording:** she stays quiet. She doesn't answer it or press anything, and doesn't talk over it. Once you speak normally, she answers you.
- If you pause for more than about a second in the middle of the recording, she may start her greeting. That's expected, so try to read it in one go.
- She mentions the **couples room**. She doesn't ask "who's your partner" in a strange way, and doesn't double the price.
- At the end she says it was **sent to the team as a request** and they will confirm. She must **not** say "you're all set".
- On the test Wix calendar the booking is **pending**, not confirmed.
